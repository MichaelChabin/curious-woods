Time Machine

Deep Time

The bar at the top is the whole of the Earth’s time, four and a half billion years, cut into sections where what we know changes. Tap a section and it comes down as a bar of its own; tap it again and it goes back up. A bar that only twitches has nothing more underneath, and that is the point. The copper marker is the year you are in: drag it, or tap a dot. Every dot is the oldest evidence of a thing, and the faint tail behind it is where the thing probably began.

The map follows the marker. This side of the ice ages it is the ground, today’s land with the sea lower where it was lower. Past them it is the earth from space, with the continents where they were, and a word under it says how sure that is, from measured down to invented. Drag the globe to turn it. Where the word is guessed, tap another guess.
