After the Ice

About twelve thousand years ago, the ice that covered the north began to melt, and it did not come back in the winter. This page counts years from then. Everything people have made since then fits on the line at the top: farms, cities, writing, ships, all of it.

The line at the top is the whole stretch of years. The pale window on it is the part you are looking at: drag it along, or drag either end. The line below spreads out the years inside the window. The copper handle is the year you are in. Drag it, or tap the line, and the map shows the world in that year, with the sea lower where it was lower. Tap a mark to read what happened there, and More for the story. People and Sea level draw a curve over the years. What I’ve seen shows only what you have tapped.
