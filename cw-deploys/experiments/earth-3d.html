The Earth today, 4,567 million years old

This is the Earth, drawn as a ball on your device’s graphics card and turned by your finger. Drag it to turn it. Pinch or scroll to come closer, all the way down to a few hundred metres across; scroll out and the whole ball comes back. Tap a place and it glides to the centre.

The line above is time: today at its left end, the Earth’s birth at its right, 4,567 million years ago. Drag the copper dot along it and the continents move to where they were, carried by their plates, and these lines say what the picture is and how sure it is.

This one needs a newer device.
