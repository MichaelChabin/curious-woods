How this worksclose
Making a number
Touch the 1 and drag to make a rectangle. Its area is the number. Tap Fix to hold it; tap Fixed to let go.
Cutting finer
Turn the dial to cut each metre into smaller pieces. Every way to make the number at that cut appears, one rectangle at a time. Tap the plane to pause; tap again to go on. Tap Every cut to try every cut up to the one on the dial.
Watching the glass
Tap a label, like , to watch the glass cut into tiles and put back together.
Counting
The numbers in the key count the ways. Turn a dot off to count without it.
Looking
Tap Near or Whole to change the view. Pinch to zoom. Tap Mirror to see all four quarters.
Glass
Tap Join to connect the corners, and Fill to fill the rectangles. Tap Color, choose a colour, then tap a row of the key.
