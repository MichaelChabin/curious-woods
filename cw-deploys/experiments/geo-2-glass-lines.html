Stained Glass Lines

The board for Wright’s Light Screens, the second geometry story. The story is not written yet; this is the tool, to find out how it feels.

The frame is your window. Touch the board once and it fills the screen; Exit at the bottom right, or the Escape key, brings the page back. Tap any empty space outside the frame to put down one dot; drag the dot to move it. Tap a dot, then tap another, and a line runs across the whole board. Where lines cross, new dots appear, and you can use those too. Tap a pane inside the frame to fill it with glass, or drag across several panes to make them one sheet. Two fingers move the board, and a pinch zooms it. Before your first line, drag a side of the frame to change its size.

Under Demo on the left, Halves finds the middle of the window with lines alone, then colours the four quarters as four sheets and shows the window by itself.

An experiment: the words in How this works are placeholders, and the frame’s numbers are in the board’s own unit, which has no name.
