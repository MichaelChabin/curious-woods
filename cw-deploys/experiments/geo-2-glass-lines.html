Stained Glass Lines

The board for Wright’s Light Screens, the second geometry story. The story is not written yet; this is the tool, to find out how it feels.

The frame is your window. Tap any empty space outside the frame to put down one dot; drag the dot to move it. Tap a dot, then tap another, and a line runs across the whole board. Where lines cross, new dots appear, and you can use those too. Tap a pane inside the frame to fill it with glass. Before your first line, drag a side of the frame to change its size.

Under Demo on the left, Halves finds the middle of the window with lines alone, and then colours it.

An experiment: the words in How this works are placeholders, and the frame’s numbers are in the board’s own unit, which has no name.
