The earth from space

Pull the sections down and drag the copper marker: the continents move to where they were in that year. Drag the globe to turn it, east and west about the pole, north and south by tilting. The globe says how sure it is, in one of five words: measured where the seafloor still carries its stripes; inferred where only rock magnetism says how far north a piece was; fitted where the pieces are real and the fits argued; guessed where the pieces are real and nobody knows where they were; invented before the oldest rock, where only the amount comes from the rocks.
