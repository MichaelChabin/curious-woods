The earth from space

Pull the sections down and drag the copper marker: the continents move to where they were in that year. Drag the globe to turn it, east and west about the pole, north and south by tilting. The globe says how sure it is: crisp where the seafloor still carries its stripes, smeared east and west where only rock magnetism and fossils say how far north a piece was, a ghost before that.
