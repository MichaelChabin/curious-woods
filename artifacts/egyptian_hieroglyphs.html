About Egyptian Hieroglyphs

How They Work: Hieroglyphs can represent sounds (phonetic), ideas (ideographic), or help clarify meaning (determinatives). Many symbols do double duty!

𓄿 = A
𓃀 = B
𓍿 = D
𓂋 = R
𓈖 = N
𓅓 = M

Cartouches: Royal names were written inside oval shapes called cartouches. The rope-like border was believed to protect the pharaoh's name.

Reading Direction: Hieroglyphs could be read left-to-right, right-to-left, or top-to-bottom. The direction of human and animal figures shows which way to read!
