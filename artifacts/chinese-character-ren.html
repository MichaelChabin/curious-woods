About this character

人 means "person" or "human"
Pronounced: rén (second tone, rising like a question)

Look at how it resembles a person walking - two legs striding forward. This is one of the oldest Chinese characters, found on oracle bones from 3,000 years ago.

Stroke order matters: Like a dance, each character has its own rhythm. Left leg first (falling left), then right leg (falling right).
