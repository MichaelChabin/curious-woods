Leonardo's Divine Proportions

Study of ideal head proportions with mathematical grid (c. 1490)

Leonardo's Mathematical Beauty

This drawing shows Leonardo's systematic approach to ideal proportions. The grid divides the head into mathematical units, revealing the hidden geometry of the human face.

The Divine Proportions:
• The face divides into three equal parts: hairline to brow, brow to nose bottom, nose to chin
• The head fits within a square from chin to top of skull
• The ear aligns with the eyebrow and nose
• These ratios appear throughout nature - Leonardo called them "divine"

Notice the characteristic left-handed hatching (diagonal lines going from lower-left to upper-right) that Leonardo used for shading. This technique creates volume and depth with just pen and ink.

"The painter who draws merely by practice and by eye, without any reason, is like a mirror which copies everything placed in front of it without being conscious of their existence." - Leonardo da Vinci
