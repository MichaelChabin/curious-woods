Leonardo's Grotesque Studies

A simplified tracing of Leonardo da Vinci's character studies (c. 1490)

About Leonardo's Grotesque Studies

Leonardo da Vinci created hundreds of grotesque heads between 1485 and 1490, studying the extremes of human physiognomy. These weren't meant to mock but to understand - Leonardo believed that understanding the extremes helped him capture normal beauty.

These drawings show Leonardo's interest in character and emotion. He wrote: "The good painter has two principal things to paint: man and the intention of his soul."

This simplified tracing captures the essential lines of Leonardo's studies - the prominent features, the characteristic profiles, and the sense of personality that made these sketches so influential on later artists.
