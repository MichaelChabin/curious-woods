Plots, before and after

22 November 2026, for Michael. Two things were built and wired into nothing, because each would change how a graph looks or where it is offered on the Time Machine: the People line with a band, a log scale and a guessed stretch back to 315,000 years (stories/curves/people-deep.json), and the sea from 540 to 3 million years ago (stories/curves/sea-level-deep.json). Each picture is the Time Machine's own drawing of the graph, taken from the page with the file handed to it by hand. Nothing here is on the page.

People

Now, on After the ice (people.json, as she sees it today)

The handle at today. The line stops at 2023; the faint dashed line is today's 8.3 billion (new on 22 Nov, with the other graphs).

Held: the same line, drawn from people-deep.json (a band and a log scale)

Each step up the side is ten times as many people. The band is the US Census Bureau's low and high.

Held: on The last ice age (130,000 years), where People is not offered today

The handle at 100,000 years ago. Before 12,000 years ago the line is the guess of Plots-Deep-01.

Held: on Mammoths (a million years)

The line begins at 315,000 years ago, with the oldest bones of our kind, and nothing is drawn before. The read-out says “about 0 million people” at 300,000 years: the page’s wording for People was written for millions, and would need a line for fewer before this is wired in.

Sea level
