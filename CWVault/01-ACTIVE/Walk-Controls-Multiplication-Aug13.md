# Controls walk — Glass Multiplication

The Geometry walk (12–13 Aug) named the species: choice panels decide and fade; tool
panels stay until closed; titles are facts, acts get words; words name destinations;
conditional presence, never greyed-out. Multiplication inherited the machinery in the
controls build — the Save panel, conditional Save, Show map ↔ Hide map. This walk
applies the same discipline to what remains, and designs the layout that has been
"interim" since the 8 Aug iPad review.

## 1. The mode bar: Multiply · Build · Explore · Properties

Four pressed-state words across the top. The walk's first question is taxonomy: are
these *viewings* (what colour and arrangement answer — the Fog ledger's per-viewing
declaration), *modes* (what gestures do), or a mix pretending to be one row? They
read as destinations, which is right, but each silently swaps the tool column's
contents (see §5) — a side effect nothing announces. The fog viewing, when it
arrives, is a viewing — does it join this row, the viewing strip, or a workshop-like
chooser? The answer decides what this row *is*.

## 2. The globals row: Fill · Continue? · Clear · Show numbers · Save

- **Clear is a destructive act with no guard.** Geometry's WIP guard (ledger §5)
  protects unsaved work everywhere including the drop; Clear here wipes the build
  with one tap. Does the guard propagate, and with what words — "forget this one"
  assumed a construction; a build may want its own noun?
- **Fill / Continue?** — the power-of-two pause (Lane 1) works; walk confirms the
  words, doesn't redo them.
- **Show numbers** duplicates part of the map's own labelling — the known Lane 1
  flag: bottom-row numerals and lattice labels both number the x axis. One of them
  yields; the walk decides which.
- **Save** is conditionally present (correct) but its panel holds only **Postcard**
  and **Full sheet** — *the record half is missing*. The standard (§2): two saves
  everywhere — the record and the artifact — and a build sequence is a record. There
  is no operation log in this lab at all today. Question with consequences: does
  Multiplication get a log (making builds replayable, steppable, shareable — the
  replay panel comes free once a log exists), or does it argue its way out in
  writing, per the standard's own escape clause?

## 3. The chips (decoder) and the tally

Chips for 1 · 2 · 3 · 5 · 7, while primes reach 19 in sound and clear glass. Are the
missing chips conditional presence (appear when 11 first lives on the glass?), a
deliberate floor, or a gap? Also: the chip *is* a control and a legend at once —
titles-are-facts says a legend is a fact; these are acts. The walk should say which
each chip is, when, and whether the tally earns its place in the column.

## 4. The build and property tool sets

Six build words (including **Diagonals ↗ / ↘** — glyphs; Geometry's walk left arrows
only inside the replay panel) and six property words. Today these appear *because a
mode was entered* — panels swapped by the mode bar, not summoned, not closable. Under
the taxonomy they are tool panels wearing mode clothing. Options: they become
summonable tool panels (the prime-toolbox idea from the species ledger §1); or the
mode bar is accepted as a chooser of tool panels and says so; or some words move to
lens panels. Also: are Properties lenses? Tapping Squares highlights a family — that
is a lens question ("one panel per question"), and it should probably speak the tap
contract's line.

## 5. The viewing strip

- **Show map ↔ Hide map** landed (inherited). Confirm, don't redo.
- **`grid follows zoom`** survives here — the state-word disease Geometry was cured
  of. Ledger §11 kept Multiplication tied *as its default* but did not rule on the
  control. Decide: destination-named toggle ("let the grid keep its step"), or no
  control at all (tied, period, until a bench argues otherwise).
- **`axes · shown`** — same disease, same options.
- The strip is also the fog viewing's likeliest socket, and each viewing must
  declare what its colour means (Fog ledger). The strip's design should leave that
  door visibly open without building it.

## 6. The readout and the number panel

The readout's resting line — "Tap a number along the bottom, then one up the left
side." — is an instruction living permanently on the surface: first-visit-as-teacher
says it earns its place once, then trusts the child. Candidate for the tip-window
treatment or conditional presence. The number description lives in the shared
cw-panel window (conforms; a lens). The audio-unsupported message is a system voice
speaking to adults — fine, but print it with the strings.

## 7. Odds and ends found in the read

- **`rebuild`** — a lowercase dev word sitting on the public page. Audit: what it
  does, whether a child should see it, whether it survives at all.
- The interim layout comment still marks the column provisional — this walk is the
  pass that removes the word "interim."
- The W compass letter (Lane 1 flag) — confirm which surface owns the fix.
- Sound permission, membrane-over-canvas, keyboard pane-cursor: believed conforming;
  confirm in passing.

## 8. Explicitly NOT this walk

Bench-first items stay benched: rotational tiling (Lane 4), the quadrant toggle
(Lane 6), the map reveal's timing (its bench is live in `experiments/`), re-stepping
at deep zoom. The 16th workshop (load-bearing accessibility) and the inner-came value
are owed builds, not walk items. Undo-inside-replay is parked in the Geometry ledger
for whichever walk reaches it first — this one may.

*Suggested order: 2 (Clear's guard and the record question — the biggest), then 1
and 4 together (they are one taxonomy question), then 5, then 3, then 6–7. Every new
child-facing string prints in full at review, both labs.*
