# Subdivision — the piece cut into p parts

A pane's piece for prime *p*, cut into *p* parts by inner cames: 2 is a piece
cut in two, 7 a piece cut in seven. Identity read by counting, no key — the
factorisation becomes something the glazier did, not something the palette
says.

Designed as the second visual channel for the Phase 4 port
(`01-ACTIVE/Design-Phase4-Aug08.md` §2, with the full argument: a count rather
than an encoding, endorsed by decision 6, the same-prime-merge finding used
deliberately, direct service to i11) and **overruled at the design review,
8 Aug 2026**: colour is the sole prime indicator, a standing principle; the
colourblind-safe workshop is the accessibility answer; workshops must declare
six distinguishable prime colours.

Parked, not discarded: **subdivision could return as a viewing of its own** —
the same panes, a different way of seeing them, where the cutting *is* the
point and counting cuts is the activity. `experiments/prime-glass.html` still
holds the working candidate next to width-by-prime, and remains the bench to
look at first.

Undecided, if it ever wakes: whether a subdivision viewing shows colour too or
goes deliberately monochrome (the strongest form of the counting argument),
and what its `name(u, v)` says a pane means.
