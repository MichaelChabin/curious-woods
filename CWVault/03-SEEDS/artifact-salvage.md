# Artifact salvage — what came back from the old folder

Roughly 23 artifacts, 8–10 months old, downloaded and inventoried 5 August 2026. A fuller
entry-by-entry inventory was produced in that session; this seed holds the parts that
change how the project runs.

**Rule for the whole folder: salvage ideas, not code.** Most of these are worksheets that
happen to run in a browser, and a worksheet stays a worksheet after every word is rewritten.
Three exceptions are noted below.

## The finding that matters most

**The Perception Lab is largely already built.** Four instruments exist as prototypes:
colour JND (with complementary, analogous, triadic, warm-cool and black-white modes
already in it), colour memory at 4% intervals, flash recognition at 200/150/100/50 ms, and
the Albers grey squares. This morning we believed one tool existed and was lost.

What they lack is not features. It is **persistence** — none of them keeps a reading, so
none is an instrument. See `S3` on the board.

## The three worth keeping as more than ideas

**Biomorphs** — nine genes, tap a creature to breed from it, the eye decides what
survives. The only file in the folder that does not talk like a teacher: no score, no
explanation, no congratulation. **Keep it as a tone reference** and study it before
writing any child-facing copy.

**Continued fractions** — two React prototypes, rectangle-peeling and coordinate-grid with
convergents. The Euclidean algorithm as a geometric act, which is the incommensurability
thread from Shortest Paths, prototyped before either of us noticed it was the same thing.
Highest-value salvage in the folder.

**Van Gogh pigments** — the chemistry is already written and correct (Prussian blue,
cobalt, chrome yellow). Cobalt is cobalt: the bridge from painting to chemistry to stained
glass to the existing palettes. Blocked on art rights, not on work.

## The tells

Every old artifact carries at least three: it announces what you are about to learn; it
asks questions it already knows the answer to; it congratulates ("Perfect!", "Reveal the
Truth", "Score: 0/0"); and it does one thing correctly and then stops, leaving nothing to
mess with once you have got the point.

That last one is the deepest and the least obvious. **Nothing to mess with** is the
difference between a demonstration and a material.

## Next

Nothing wholesale. Migration is opportunistic: when a session touches one of these, it
becomes its own seed and this list loses a line. A salvage project would produce worse
seeds than writing them when they matter.
