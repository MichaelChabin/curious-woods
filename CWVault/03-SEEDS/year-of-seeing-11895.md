# 1895 — the year of seeing

Not a story yet. A holding note for the cluster, because the coincidence is too good to
leave to chance discovery.

Within a few months of each other: Röntgen makes an image through his wife's hand in
Würzburg and does not know what the rays are, so he calls them X. The Lumière brothers
project moving pictures to a paying audience in a Paris basement in December — two months
after a real locomotive had come through a real wall a few kilometres away. Marconi sends
a signal through the air with nothing carrying it.

Three different ways of seeing what could not previously be seen, in one year, none of them
aware of the others.

## Why hold it as a cluster

Any single one of these is a decent story. Together they are a *sense of historical
sweep* (i13) — the feeling that a year is a room with several things happening in it, and
that the people inside could not see each other. That feeling is not teachable by a
timeline. It comes from standing in the room.

## What it opens onto

Frequency as the thread nobody expects: X-rays, visible light, and radio are the same
phenomenon at different frequencies, and 1895 is the year humans learned to use all three
without knowing that. That is a genuinely startling fact and it is *true*, which makes it
the kind of thing worth an anchor story rather than a mention.

Connects onward to the Perception Lab — the child measuring which frequencies of light she
can tell apart is standing on the visible slice of the same axis.
