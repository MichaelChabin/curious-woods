# The spread of languages and technologies: periods with regions

Michael, 1 Oct 2026: the Time Machine as a standalone app for exploring the emergence and disappearance of languages and the spread of technologies. In the shape of the tool these are *periods with regions* (Time-Machine-Shape.md): a span and a region that changes with the year, drawn as a wash that comes and goes. This is the one addition the engine still needs; the washes exist (map.js `region` marks), the year-driven layers exist, and what is missing is a region with a from and a to. Undecided: the data shape (one region per step, or a region that interpolates), and the first case to build it on.
