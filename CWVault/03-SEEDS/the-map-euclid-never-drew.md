# The map Euclid never drew

The grid is a construction. Every lattice point is compass-and-straightedge
constructible — drop a perpendicular, transfer the unit, repeat — so Euclid *could*
have drawn the coordinate plane. He never did. The marriage of grid and geometry
waited roughly two millennia for Descartes (La Géométrie, 1637 CE — era 11637), and
its absence is part of why some things were so hard for the Greeks.

## Where this came from

The controls walk, 12 August 2026. Michael, on the map toggle: turning grids on and
off is so natural that a child could forget Euclid could have done it. The immediate
answer is built into the lab rather than told — the map assembles itself by
construction once per session when she asks for it
(`01-ACTIVE/Decisions-Controls-Aug12.md` §8). This seed holds the part that wants to
be a *story*.

  right order.
