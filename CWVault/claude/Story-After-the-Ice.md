# After the Ice

About twelve thousand years ago, the ice that covered the north began to melt, and it did not come back in the winter. This page counts years from then. Everything people have made since then fits on the line at the top: farms, cities, writing, ships, all of it.

The line at the top is the whole twelve thousand years. The pale blue window on that line is the part you are looking at now. Drag the window to move through time. Drag either end of the window to make it wider or narrower.

The line at the bottom shows what happened inside the window. Tap a mark on the bottom line, and the map moves to the place where that thing happened. Tap a place on the map, and its mark lights up on the bottom line. The words between the line and the map say what happened there.

The map moves too. Drag it to travel. Pinch it, or scroll, to come closer.

The road in the middle is the same years again, drawn so that the years nearest to you get the most room.

*What I've seen* shows only the things you have already tapped.

## Notes

**The beat.** She can hold twelve thousand years in one hand. Nothing else.

**Before it goes on the page.** Two words must match the screen: "mark" (dot or diamond, whichever the bottom line draws) and "between the line and the map" (wherever the panel actually sits; if it is under the map, say "under the map"). *What I've seen* must be the exact word on the page.

**What it never says.** That this is interesting. That she will learn anything. What she should look for.

**Gallery picture.** No icon exists yet. The page borrows `art/icons/map-icon-256.png`. Wanted: a drawn mark, square, in the house line — a short horizontal line with a copper dot on it, or the pale blue window on a line — so it hangs beside the star and the three dots, not beside the paintings.
