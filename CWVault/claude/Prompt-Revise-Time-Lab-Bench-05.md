# The Time lab bench, step 5: the labels and the menu

`cw-deploys/experiments/time-lab.html` only. Read Report-Time-Lab-Bench-04.md first.

## Years are written short

Everywhere the page writes a year that counts back from now (the flags, the handle, line one, the cursor label, the mosaics' own labels):

- a billion years ago and older: **Ba** (*4.6 Ba*)
- a million to a billion: **Ma** (*720 Ma*, *66 Ma*)
- 12,000 years to a million: **ka** (*474 ka*)
- inside the last 12,000 years: the calendar year (*200 BCE*, *1720*)
- the present: *now*, not *today*

So the handle reads *252 Ma · inferred*, and line one for a period reads *Dinosaurs · 252 to 66 Ma · 186 million years*.

A length stays in words: *719 million years*, *six thousand years*. Ma means "ago"; a length is not a date.

The store's prose is not touched. Where line one takes an event's date from its summary (*Archaeopteryx · about 149 million years ago*), write it short if that is one rule in one place (*about 149 Ma*); if it is not, leave it and say so.

## The flags

- **The year stands outside its flag, outward:** left of the left flag, right of the right flag. Neither label may cover its flag or its pennant. (In the page as it stands the left flag is hidden by its own year.)
- **No year twice.** When the handle stands on a flag, the handle's label is the only one; the flag's is not drawn.
- **The span moves up.** The length between the flags (*719 million years*) leaves the line and sits at the level of the flags' labels, centred between the two flags. The line under it is unbroken.
- Step 4's rules for collisions go where these make them needless. Where a short line leaves no room for the span between the flags, say in the report what you did.

## A selection through the last tile ends at now

With the 4.6 billion mosaic showing, select the last 720 million years by dragging through the final tile: the right flag reads *1 million years ago*, the older edge of that tile. It should read *now*. A selection that includes a mosaic's last tile ends at now, on every mosaic, however it was made (a tap on a period, a drag, a pull).

## focus and back

Both words move up and to the right, so that no event's stem or label on the line can write over them. Their meanings are unchanged: *focus* appears when the flags have been moved in, and stretches that span to the whole line; *back* appears once anything has been selected, and undoes the last selection.

## The menu

*How this works* stays at the top, its text as it is. Michael will send new words; do not rewrite them.

Under it, two groups, in the house convention:

**Earth**

- *Full screen*
- *Large Earth* / *Small Earth* / *Map*: three words, the one in use in copper, as the scales are. *Map* is the flat world.
- *Reset*

**Information**

- *Hide periods* is deleted, with its code. Pointing lifts the period and the text under the Earth tells it; nothing dims.
- *Show cities* / *Hide cities*
- *Show coastlines* / *Hide coastlines*: today's coastlines drawn on the Earth at any age, riding their plates as the cities do. If the page has no coastline data that can ride the plates, stop at this item and report what there is.
- *Place markers* / *Hide markers*: with *Place markers* chosen, a tap on the Earth sets a marker there, and she may set as many as she likes. A marker rides its plate as the year changes, so she can see where that spot travels through time. A tap on a marker deletes it. A drag still turns the Earth. *Hide markers* hides them and ends the placing; they come back when she chooses *Place markers* again. Say what a marker does at an age when its ground did not yet exist.

The bench's own switches in *How this works* (small / large and the rest) go where the menu now does their work.

## Select years moves down

*Select years* and its scale words leave the top of the column and sit to the left of the mosaic, centred on the mosaic's height, staying so when the mosaic changes. The three scales are unchanged in this step.
