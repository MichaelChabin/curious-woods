## Batch 6: the oldest art, music and ornaments

Michael, 7 Oct: these, with both Spanish caves as marks of their own, "because we can find images." Pictures matter for this batch: say in each event's *Pictures wanted* what exists and under what licence.

1. **Shell beads**, Morocco, perhaps about 140,000 years ago (Bizmoune Cave; Grotte des Pigeons is younger, about 82,000). How old the oldest beads are is argued.
2. **Blombos Cave**, South Africa, about 75,000: ochre with crossed lines cut into it, and shell beads.
3. **Sulawesi**, Indonesia: hand stencils and a painted pig, 50,000 years or more; the oldest dated cave paintings found, older than any in Europe. Check for 2025–2026 redatings.
4. **Lebombo bone**, Border Cave, on the South Africa–Eswatini border, about 43,000: a notched bone.
5. **Flutes**, Hohle Fels and Geissenklösterle, Swabian Jura, Germany, about 40,000: bird bone and mammoth ivory. The oldest instruments found. (The Divje Babe "flute", Slovenia, said to be Neanderthal, is argued; it may belong in the More.)
6. **Venus of Hohle Fels**, Germany, about 40,000: the oldest carving of a person found. May share a mark with the flutes if the research says so; ask Michael.
7. **El Castillo**, Cantabria, Spain: a red disc dated to about 40,000, and the claim (2018) that Neanderthals painted in nearby caves, argued.
8. **Chauvet**, Ardèche, France, about 36,000: lions and horses.
9. **Altamira**, Cantabria, Spain: the bison; the ceiling's age and the story of its finding, and of not being believed, to be checked.
10. **Lady of Brassempouy**, the Landes, southwestern France, about 25,000: the small ivory head with the hood (Michael's "ivory carving of a woman with a headdress").
11. **Ishango bone**, Democratic Republic of the Congo, about 20,000 (the date has moved; check): notches read as counting, primes or a moon calendar, all argued. The bar's first mark about numbers. Kept in Brussels.
12. **Lascaux**, Dordogne, France, about 17,000.

Kinds: mostly *object* or *place*. Place now is where the thing is found or kept; for a cave closed to visitors (Lascaux, Chauvet, Altamira) say so, and name the replica. Write for a child in each place: Morocco, South Africa, Eswatini, Sulawesi, the Swabian Jura, Cantabria, the Ardèche, the Landes, the Congo, the Dordogne.
