## First trilobites

**Label:** First trilobites
**Age:** 521 Ma · ± 2 Ma · estimate: the oldest trilobites are placed by their layer, between beds of volcanic ash dated elsewhere; no date on the fossils' own rock
**Tail:** none
**Known from:** armour of calcite, kept in shale and limestone
**Place now:** the middle Lena River, Sakha (Yakutia), Siberia, Russia (about 61 N, 127 E, approximate), where *Profallotaspis* was found. Others as old come from Morocco, Spain, and the Nevada–California border.
**Kind:** a crop or animal
**Replaces:** nothing; new

**Summary (35 words)**
About 521 million years ago. Trilobites, armoured sea animals with many legs and eyes of crystal, turn up in rock in Siberia, Morocco and Nevada, already different from one another. No older rock holds one. *More*

**More (327 words)**

```
About 521 million years ago.
```

Split the right piece of shale on a hillside in Nevada, in Morocco, or along the Lena River in Siberia, and an animal may be looking back at you. It has a head like a half moon, a body of many jointed pieces, and two eyes. It is a trilobite ("TRY-luh-bite").

The name means three lobes. Look along its back and there they are: a raised middle running from head to tail, and a flatter part on each side.

Trilobites lived in the sea and walked the bottom on many pairs of legs. Their armour was calcite, the mineral that limestone and seashells are made of. So were the lenses of their eyes. A trilobite saw the world through crystals of stone, and because stone keeps, the lenses can still be counted.

Here is the strange part. Rock a little older holds tracks, burrows and tiny shells, but no trilobites at all. Then, in the layers just above, there they are: in Siberia, in Morocco, in Spain, in Nevada. And they are not all alike. The first ones in Siberia are a different kind from the first ones in Nevada.

Charles Darwin knew this, and it bothered him. Animals so complicated, he thought, must have had a long line of ancestors. Where were they?

There are two answers, and people still argue. One is that the ancestors had no armour. Soft animals almost never turn to stone, so the rock kept nothing until the armour came. The other, from a study in 2022, is that there was no long line to lose: trilobites really did begin about then, and spread through the seas quickly.

Nobody has dated a first trilobite itself. Its age comes from layers of volcanic ash above and below such fossils, whose crystals can be dated. They say about 521 million years, give or take a million or two.

Trilobites went on for almost 270 million years. More than 22,000 kinds have been named.

**Notes for us.** The beat: an animal with crystal eyes, there all at once on several continents, and an argument about why that Darwin started and nobody has finished. Told as argued, not settled, as *Dickinsonia* is. It is the lab's opening moment, so it must stand on its own for a child who has read nothing else. From the one search: first appearance about 521 Ma, marking the base of Cambrian Series 2 and Stage 3 (the Atdabanian in Siberia); contenders *Profallotaspis jakutensis* (Siberia), *Fritzaspis* (Esmeralda Basin, western Nevada and eastern California), *Hupetina antiqua* (Morocco), *Serrania gordaensis* (Spain); already diverse and widely spread when they appear; last trilobites about 251.9 Ma; over 22,000 species described; the 2022 paper concludes that the appearance at about 521 Ma closely reflects the group's origin. The neighbours: *Animals, fossils* (539 Ma) already says "by about 520 million years ago trilobites"; *The map clears* (500 Ma) uses trilobites to draw the map. Left out: the names of the genera (four hard words in a first screen); *Rusophycus*, the resting traces older than any trilobite body, which bear on the argument; the head, thorax and tail, which children are usually told are the three lobes and are not. For the checkers: the place and coordinates for *Profallotaspis* (which section on the Lena; the figure here is a guess at the region); whether "521" or "about 520" is the better figure, and what the ± should be; "between beds of volcanic ash dated elsewhere" (which beds; Morocco's ash dates are the likely ones); the size of the first trilobites (the draft gives none); "a head like a half moon"; "many pairs of legs" (legs are known from later trilobites, not these); calcite lenses in the very first trilobites, not only later ones; "the first ones in Siberia are a different kind from the first ones in Nevada" (*Profallotaspis?* is also reported from Nevada); "tracks, burrows and tiny shells" in the rock just below; what Darwin wrote, and whether "it bothered him" is fair; the 2022 study's authors and whether the draft puts its conclusion fairly; "almost 270 million years"; Hollingsworth 2008's title and volume; **Kind**, where the closed list offers nothing nearer than "a crop or animal".
