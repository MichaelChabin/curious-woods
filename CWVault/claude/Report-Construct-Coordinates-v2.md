# Report: Construct Coordinates, second bench

## What it is

v1's page, kept whole, with the six things the prompt asks for. Every construction is still data in a log of steps, drawing separate from the log; the shelf is untouched (`glass.js` not opened). The log has three new kinds of step beside v1's `pt`, `line`, `arc` and `lab`: `hold` (nothing drawn, time passes), `flab` (a fraction, written), and a `line` that grows from any point in any direction, which the fraction's line needs and the grid's lines use too.

**Kept from v1:** the opening from the seeds 0 and 1, full circles only, the legible opening and the accelerando with their knobs, ceremony that fades once its work is done, tap skips ahead, deep zoom constructs the tenths, v1's palette and type.

**1. Numbers as they are minted.** A point on an axis pushes its number into the log right behind it, so 1 is written while the seeds are the only thing on the screen and −1 as its circle mints it. The labels-last block is gone. A number takes no slot in the legible opening: the six legible steps are the same six constructions as in v1.

**2. The pause at the y axis.** After the two radius-2 circles and the point at (0, √3), a hold before the y axis is drawn; default 0.5 s, a knob *pause at the y axis* (0 to 2 s). The two circles wait out the hold before they fade, so what she sees during the pause is the crossing with both circles still there (`op-04-pause-at-sqrt3.jpg`). A hold of 0 puts no step in the log.

**3. The first grid lines, slowly.** The first *n* grid lines (default 3, knob *slow grid lines*, 0 to 8) are built at full weight, outside the accelerando, each followed by a hold (default 0.4 s, knob *hold after each*, 0 to 1.5 s); their circles wait out the hold too. The holds are added to the run: at the defaults the run is 5.0 s + 0.5 + 3 × 0.4 = 6.7 s, and the readout says so. In v1's order the first three lines are x = 1, x = −1 and x = 2, all vertical (`op-07` to `op-09`); see *Open*.

**4. The axis labels.** *x axis* under the x axis near the right edge; *y axis* left of the y axis near the top; v1's number type at 0.7 opacity. They appear as their axis is drawn, ride it on pan and zoom, clamp to the axis's own end where the axis is shorter than the screen, and go when the axis's line leaves the screen. Each frame the page keeps a list of everything it drew (every line as a segment, every circle still visible as a ring, every point, every number, the fraction labels, the panel's box) and the label slides along its axis to the nearest spot that touches none of it, easing there over about half a second rather than jumping, with a few pixels of give so a passing circle does not make it twitch. Where nothing on the screen is clear (the lowest zooms, where the grid is 22 px; under the finer grid) it takes the spot that covers least, a grid line before a number, and pays for distance from its edge so it stays near it.

**5. Fractions.** Once the grid stands, a tap within 14 px of a crossing of the main grid (both coordinates whole, neither zero, inside the built grid) starts one; a tap on an axis point, off a crossing, or on a crossing already taken does nothing. f = |y| / |x|. The log, in order: the line through the point and (0, 0) across the plane; the point where it crosses x = 1, at (1, f) or (1, −f) in the second and fourth quadrants; the circle centred on (1, 0) through it, with its two marks at 1 − f and 1 + f; then the carry by the method on the switch; then the fraction written. *walk from 1*: circles each centred on the last mark and passing through the one before, outward from 1 + f and 1 − f to the screen's edge, and on the y axis a circle centred on (0, 1) with radius f, then the same walk. *carry to 0*: one circle centred on (0, 0) with radius f marks both axes at f, then the walk from 0. Circles fade as in the opening; the marks stay; no labels on the marks. The fraction is stacked by `cw-number.js` (the number standard: numerator over a bar over denominator, the bar on the math axis), at 12 px, to the left of the tapped point with the bar at the point's height; not in lowest terms it is written as tapped and then reduced, so (4, 2) shows *2/4, 1/2*. All of it in the one copper of `cw-style.js`. Several stand at once; a tap while one builds skips to its end (with *tap does nothing* set, a tap on another crossing queues it instead). One fraction takes 3 s by default (knob *one fraction takes*, 1 to 8 s): its first three steps (the line, the crossing, the circle) take half that time at full weight, the walk the other half under the main run's acceleration knob — a hundred walk circles would otherwise leave the three steps that carry the idea a blink.

**6. Clear.** *clear fractions* in the panel removes every fraction and nothing else. *replay* rebuilds the grid at the current view and then every standing fraction after it, each as it was built, in the order they were tapped (one clock; the main run at offset 0, each fraction at its own offset after the last).

**Also, because the prompt's check needs it:** v1 had no pan. One finger (or the mouse) drags the plane; two fingers pinch; the wheel and the zoom knob zoom as before; a tap is a pointer that did not move more than 6 px.

**Bench only:** `window.cwConstruct` (the log, the clock, `freeze(seconds)` to hold the clock for a picture, `seek`, the view, the obstacle list and the labels' state) — how the checks below were made. The bench note at the bottom now sits right of the panel so the two do not overlap on a short window.

## For Michael's eye

- **The pauses.** At the default knobs on this window the opening before the pause is half a second (ten steps), then 0.5 s of stillness; whether the pause reads as a pause or as a stall is the thing to look at, and the knob goes to 2 s. The three slow lines each hold 0.4 s; the step from the last hold into the accelerando is abrupt by design.
- **Where the labels slide to.** At rest, *x axis* between 8 and 9 and *y axis* under the 7; at 22 px per unit nothing is clear and the words cover a number at the edge; under the tenths they sit over the tenths. Whether "a little quieter" at 0.7 is quiet enough.
- **The two methods on 2/3.** `walk-3-2-c-done.jpg` against `carry-3-2-c-done.jpg`: the walk from 1 never lands on 2/3 itself; the carry to 0 does. The switch applies to fractions tapped after it; the ones standing stay as they were built.
- **The fraction's label** at 12 px, with the stacked digits at 10 px; and its place, left of the point with the bar at the point's height.
