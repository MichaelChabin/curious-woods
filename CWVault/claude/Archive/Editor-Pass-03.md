**The verdict.** Both stand, nearly as they are. I found no fault of fact in either. The changes are a handful of words and three Watch lines.

**Why these are so much cleaner than batches 1 to 3.** Two things changed on 1 October. You rewrote *Pythagoras*, and the prose learned to run. And the list of traps went into the rulebook, so the writers and checkers were looking for legends, old figures and overclaims from the start. ## Left alone, on purpose

- *Pythagoras* is Michael's own text. Not touched.
- *Jerwan*: "three grown men" is not "grown-ups"; it is how anyone would say it.
- *Enheduanna*, *Nineveh's library*, *First alphabet*, *Gutenberg* (the Korean book): each says plainly that the object is now in Philadelphia, London or Paris, without saying how it got there. Unlike the Diamond Sutra, none of these Mores is about the object's finding and removal, and a sentence on it would be a second story. Worth a ruling some day: when does an object's journey to a foreign museum earn its sentence?
- *Confucius*, *Paper*, *Compass*: the pronunciations are rough but serviceable; a Chinese speaker should hear them before they are ever read aloud.
