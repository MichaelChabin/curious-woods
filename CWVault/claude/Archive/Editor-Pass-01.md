# Editor pass 1

## The page for Michael

**What was read.** Batch 1 of the After the Ice events, all twenty, each More read as her and then as a checker. Then a script over the whole backlog: nineteen files, 224 entries.

**The verdict on batch 1.** Good, and ready to stand once the fixes below are in. Seventeen of twenty need nothing or a word. Three had a real fault, each now checked against a source:

- *Nok heads* said the iron "ran out" of the furnace. It did not. Those furnaces never got hot enough to melt iron; the iron formed as a solid lump and only the waste ran.
- *Kalinga war* quotes Ashoka in Romila Thapar's translation, but not in her words. The house rule is word for word or no quotation marks.
- *Great Zimbabwe* says "perhaps ten thousand people". A 2017 study by Shadreck Chirikure and others argues for far fewer, and another archaeologist argues for more. The number is now told as an argument.

**One thing I decided that you may want to overrule.** *Diamond Sutra* says the scroll was found in a cave in China and "is now in the British Library in London", and leaves out how it got there. A child in China knows how. By your *Who is reading* rule I am adding one plain sentence: in 1907 Aurel Stein bought it from the caretaker, with thousands of other scrolls, for a small sum. Say no and it comes out.

**9. Rhind papyrus.** The opening quotation has no named translator (the notes say so), and the rule is a named translation or a paraphrase. Replace the second sentence, from "It begins with a promise:" to the closing quotation mark, with: It begins with a promise. **10. Lapita voyagers.** "they reached Hawaii, New Zealand and Easter Island." becomes "they reached Hawaiʻi, Aotearoa (New Zealand) and Rapa Nui (Easter Island)." (Who is reading: the names people there use. Batch 5 already writes Hawaiʻi and Aotearoa.)

**11. Nok heads.** "heating rock in clay furnaces until the metal ran out" becomes "heating iron ore with charcoal in clay furnaces until a lump of iron formed inside". Add to the notes: bloomery smelting; the iron is never molten (Historical Metallurgy Society, Datasheet 301).

**13. Kalinga war.** The quotation is brought to Thapar's wording: "One hundred and fifty thousand people" becomes "A hundred and fifty thousand people"; "one hundred thousand were killed, and many" becomes "a hundred thousand were killed and many". **15. Antikythera.** In the summary, "A Greek ship sank carrying" becomes "A ship sank off Greece carrying" (whose ship it was is not known). In the More, "a scientist named Derek de Solla Price" becomes "a historian of science named Derek de Solla Price".

**18. Add a Watch line: where the scroll is kept · last checked 15 Nov 2026 · every year.

**20. "from a Shona word for houses of stone" becomes "from Shona words for houses of stone". Add to the references: Shadreck Chirikure and others, "What was the population of Great Zimbabwe (CE1000 – 1800)?", *PLoS ONE*, 2017.

**6. Campo del Cielo.** No change to the words. Add a Watch line: Gancedo third-heaviest meteorite found · last checked 15 Nov 2026 · every year.

- *Eratosthenes*: Syene is not quite on the tropic or due south. The notes already say why that stays out.
