# The Time lab bench, step 7: Michael's adjustments

`cw-deploys/experiments/time-lab.html` only. Read Report-Time-Lab-Bench-06.md first. Where this prompt and step 6 differ, this prompt rules.

## The Earth: one word, three views

*Large Earth*, *Small Earth* and *Map* become one word in the menu. The word names the view she will get next, in a fixed circle:

| Showing | The word reads |
|---|---|
| Small Earth | *Large Earth* |
| Large Earth | *Map* |
| Map | *Small Earth* |

The page opens on Small Earth, as now. *Full screen* and *Reset* stay as they are.

## The mosaic: shorter

Vertical space is scarce. Small tiles are fine as long as the mosaic still gives a useful sense of scale.

- The *4.6 billion* mosaic keeps its five rows.
- **Every other mosaic, and every gathered selection, is as wide as the timeline and no taller than one row of the *4.6 billion* mosaic.** Shrink the tiles, or merge them by tens as step 6 allows, to make that true. The reminder line still says what a tile is.
- Say in the report the tile size and unit you ended with for each of the eight spans.

## Select and back

Today a tap on a period gathers it at once. Change that to two steps.

1. She taps a period, or drags across tiles. The choice is marked on the mosaic, and nothing else moves.
2. The word *select* appears at the right of the mosaic.
3. She taps *select*. The chosen tiles gather into their line over the timeline, as in step 6. The names of the periods inside the selection show above that line (*Dinosaurs*, *Farms*, and so on), as they do above *Civilization* now. *select* turns into *back*.
4. She taps *back*. The tiles travel back to where they came from, and the view she had before is restored exactly.

- There is one *back* word on the page, at the right of the mosaic. It undoes the last step, whether that step was a *select* or a *focus* on the timeline. *focus* stays at the right of the timeline. (This is Claude's reading; say so in the report if it fights with anything.)
- If she taps a different period before tapping *select*, the mark moves to it. A tap on empty ground clears the mark and hides *select*.
- Choosing a word under *Select years* needs no *select*: it acts at once, as now.

## The move: slower, and both ways

- The shuffle takes about twice as long as it does in step 6. Give the old and new times in the report.
- It plays in both directions everywhere: on *select*, on *back*, on going into a smaller mosaic under *Select years*, and on going out to a larger one (*Civilization* to *4.6 billion*, say). Going out is the going-in move reversed: the tiles on screen travel to their places in the larger mosaic while the rest appear around them.
- If the two spans don't nest (*Paper* to *Complex life* is fine; they all nest in *4.6 billion*, but check each pair), cut, and say which pairs cut.
- The sound stretches to match the longer move. Reduced motion is still a cut with no sound.

## Full screen on the iPad

On the iPad, a downward swipe leaves full screen, and a two-finger drag downward is how she zooms. So she falls out of full screen while using the Earth.

Find out whether that swipe can be stopped while the page uses the browser's own full screen. It probably cannot; it looks like the system's gesture, not the page's. If it cannot, make *Full screen* on touch devices a full page instead: the lab fills the whole browser window without calling the browser's full screen, and *Full screen* turns into *Leave full screen*. Keep the browser's own full screen on the Mac.

Report what you found and which way you went. Test on the iPad itself if you can; if you can't, say so plainly.

## The text between the Earth and the mosaic

There are three text areas now, and they change independently. Make them two, driven by one thing: what she has selected.

**Remove** the line that gives the age of the whole (*The Solar System · 4,600 million years*, and its kin on other spans). The period area now does that job.

**The period area**, on the left. Its left edge lines up with the left edge of the mosaic, so the two read as one thing.

- First line: the span of what she is looking at, and its length.
- Then the name in bold and the description.
- With nothing selected, it describes the whole mosaic in front of her. With a period selected, it describes that period.

Examples of the shape:

> 4.6 billion years ago to now
> **The Earth.** The history of the Earth, from the Sun's first light to this morning.

> 470 to 252 Ma · 218 million years
> **Life on land.** Plants and animals come to land.

Period descriptions come from the store, as now. For the eight *Select years* spans, use these drafts. Do not improve them; Michael will.

| Span | Description |
|---|---|
| *4.6 billion* | The history of the Earth, from the Sun's first light to this morning. |
| *Complex life* | From the great freeze to now. Animals appear, grow, and fill the sea, the land and the air. |
| *Humans* | The last million years. The ice comes and goes, and people keep fires and make tools. |
| *Civilization* | The last 12,000 years. The ice melts, and people begin to farm and build towns. |
| *Writing* | Since about 3300 BCE, people have been leaving words for other people to read. |
| *Paper* | Since about 200 BCE, words have been cheap enough to copy and carry. |
| *Machines* | Since about 1720, engines have been doing work that muscles used to do. |
| *Computing* | Since about 1950, machines have been doing arithmetic, and then a good deal more. |

**The event area**, on the right, is for events only.

- First line: the date and the event's label (*About 375 Ma · Tiktaalik*).
- Then the summary, ending in *More*, as now.
- Empty when no event is chosen. Nothing else writes there.

Neither area may change the place of anything below it as its text grows or shrinks. Give each a fixed height that holds the longest text in the store, and say what that height is.

## Years ago, or years from the start

One new word in the menu, under **Information**: *years from the start*. Tapped, it turns into *years ago*, and back again.

- **Years ago** is how the page counts now, and it is the default.
- **Years from the start** counts forward from the first tile of the *4.6 billion* mosaic. A span she selects begins at its distance from that start. *Life on land* would read *4.13 to 4.35 billion years from the start · 218 million years*.
- The word governs the first line of the period area, the labels on the flags and the handle, the labels at the ends of the mosaic's rows, and the date on an event's first line.
- Numbers counted from the start stay round: three figures at most.
- Inside the last 12,000 years, calendar years stay calendar years in both modes.
- The length between two flags is the same in both modes and stays in words.

This item comes from a conversation on 16 Nov and Michael may strike it. Build it so that it can be removed cleanly.
