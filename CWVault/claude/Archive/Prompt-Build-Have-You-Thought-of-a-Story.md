Put the caption in the left column, inside the figure, and centre the whole left-column block on the picture's height rather than aligning it with the picture's foot. Keep the zoom note above the caption if it fits without pushing the block off centre; if it doesn't, drop the note, since the word below the caption now says what to do. On a phone, where the column folds into the text, the block sits under the picture as the shared stylesheet already does. Do this with a rule that a page can opt into (a class on the figure, e.g. `figure.tall`, in `css/story.css`), not with page-only CSS: the page still sets only `--aspect`.
>
> **2. One bold word under the caption, on its own line.** *Tap the page to see a magnifier.* While the magnifier is out, the same word reads *Put the magnifier away*, and tapping it puts the magnifier away. It replaces the shared *Magnifier off* word on this page; *The whole picture* stays where it is for pinch. Bold, the `.word` style. The page's own instruction sentence, "Tap the page, and a magnifier appears there," is deleted.
>
> **3. The heading *The page* is deleted.** The text runs straight from the picture into "This is a page from a notebook."
>
> **4. More, five edits, from the story file as it now stands (Draft 2.3); take the wording from there, not from here.** *The name*: reworded. *The volcano*: three sentences now, and the loudest-sound claim is gone. *Darkness* moves down to sit after *Ada*, with its own bold lead **Darkness.**, one new sentence, and its word renamed *Read Byron's Darkness* (the `#readPoem` button's text; the poem behind it stays). *Ada*: one sentence added. *Two books*: "The book Mary wrote".
