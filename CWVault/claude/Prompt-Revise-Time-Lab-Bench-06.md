# The Time lab bench, step 6: Select years, and the mosaic gathers

`cw-deploys/experiments/time-lab.html` only. Read Report-Time-Lab-Bench-05.md first.

## Select years

Eight spans replace the three scales. *Last million* and *Last 12,000* are gone into *Humans* and *Civilization*.

| Word | From | A tile is |
|---|---|---|
| *4.6 billion* | the Sun's birth | a million years |
| *Complex life* | 720 Ma, the start of the glaciations | a million years |
| *Humans* | a million years ago | a thousand years |
| *Civilization* | 12,000 years ago | ten years |
| *Writing* | the earliest writing, about 3300 BCE | ten years |
| *Paper* | about 200 BCE | ten years |
| *Machines* | about 1720 | one year |
| *Computing* | about 1950 | one year |

Every span runs to now.

- *4.6 billion*, *Humans* and *Civilization* are the mosaics the page has.
- **Each of the others is its own mosaic, as wide as the timeline:** its tiles laid in as many rows as make the block span the line's width at a sensible tile size, reading as the 4.6 billion does. Say in the report the rows and tile size you chose for each.
- A tile keeps the colour of the period it falls in. Where a span has no periods of its own yet (*Machines* and *Computing* sit inside two of the 12,000's), say what you drew.
- **Civilization:** the last three decades stand as three tiles by themselves at the right end, apart from the block, not as part of a larger square.
- The reminder line under each mosaic says its unit (*Each tile is ten years*).
- The line, the flags, the handle, line one and the events of the store behave on every span as they do now. Years are written as step 5 rules: calendar years inside the last 12,000.

## The mosaic gathers

When she selects a period on the mosaic in front of her:

1. the tiles that are not in it vanish, in a way worth watching;
2. the tiles that are in it resize if they must and travel to a single line directly above the timeline, in time order, oldest at the left, keeping their colours.

That line of tiles then stands above the timeline for as long as the selection does.

- If the period's tiles will not fit one line at any readable size, they merge by tens before they travel, as Worknote-Mosaic-Focus-01 describes, and the reminder line says the new unit.
- **Choosing a word under *Select years*** plays the same move when the new span lies inside the mosaic showing (*Complex life* from *4.6 billion*; *Writing* from *Civilization*): its tiles stay, the rest vanish, and they re-form as the new mosaic. Otherwise a cut.
- **It is quick.** Under a second, start to finish. If it cannot be made quick for a large selection, cut instead and say where the line fell.
- ***back*** undoes the last selection, and the move plays in reverse. *focus* is not part of this: it belongs to the flags on the line, as in step 5.
- A drag across tiles selects a span as it does now; say whether it gathers too, and show both.
- Reduced motion: a cut, both ways, and no sound.

Offer two or three ways for the unwanted tiles to vanish, behind a bench switch, for Michael to choose from.

## The sound

A soft shuffling sound as the tiles travel, ending as the last one lands. One sound for the move, not one per tile. Under Ruling-Sound-01: silent until she has answered *Sound OK?*, and a *sound on* / *sound off* word in the menu, under **Information**. Three candidate sounds behind a bench switch, for Michael to hear.

## Not in this step

The 13.8 rung; trips; the probe; *Cut open*; the front door; the swap and the retiring of the Time Machine and After the Ice.
