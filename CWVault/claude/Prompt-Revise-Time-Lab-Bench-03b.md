## One block of text under the Earth

- **Line one, always:** the year and the word of knowing. "12,026 years ago · *measured*". "252 million years ago · *inferred*". The word in copper, as the grade word is now. The three words are *measured*, *inferred* and *guessed* (Lab-Time, the words of knowing, ruled 4 Nov): *ground* and *measured* show as *measured*; *inferred* and *fitted* as *inferred*; *guessed* and *invented* as *guessed*; *none* shows no word. - **Lines two to four:** whichever she touched last — a period's box words (pointing at the mosaic, or the selected period once she lifts), or an event's summary ending in *More* (tapping the line). - The summary block that step 1 put under the timeline goes; the summary lives here now. *More* opens the window as before.

## The mosaics' labels

- **The reminder line** above each mosaic ("Each tile is …") is set in the size of the number labels and sits tight to the mosaic, the gap no more than the labels' own line height.
- **The year labels on the right of the 4.6 row** keep counting forward ("1 billion", "2 billion", …) and are set small; the last label, where "4.6 billion" sits, reads **Now**. No abbreviations.
- **The mosaic is centred on the column**, the same axis as the Earth; the year labels hang outside it to the right and don't count toward centring.
- **The 12,000 row loses its thousand-year numbers.** The blocks are the ruler; the period names under the row are the labels. Names alone under the row, no durations; a period's length belongs in its box words ("Farms. Six thousand years. The ice is gone …") and is said there.
- **The 12,000 row's right end:** the current year, **2026**, set to the right of the row in the house text, with a hairline from it to the gold tile at now, the row's only number. *Electricity*'s name goes beside the year, to the right of the row, not under it; *Machines* stays under the row. The clear tiles of the unfinished block stay, and the count of lit tiles comes from today's date, so the row fills itself a tile a decade.
- **The last million gets periods**, as names under the row the way the 12,000 has them, while the tiles keep the ice and warm spells as their colour. No brackets: a name centred under its span. The box words carry "about" where an edge is rough. *Placeholder* names and spans, Michael's to change: **Hand axes** (1,000,000 to 600,000 years ago), **Fire** (600,000 to 300,000), **Neanderthals** (300,000 to 50,000), **Painted caves** (50,000 to 12,000).
