# R2: one frame

Built in the working tree on top of the tidying's commits (HEAD dbe2757); `js/glass.js` is 6,017 lines, from 5,923. Stamps `2026-10-24f dbe2757`, `?v=2026-10-24f` on the three pages.

## What changed

**The frame is the shell's.** A new section of the record, *The frame*, holds it: a rectangle on the plane in the board's unit (a Wright step, 56 world units, until R3 makes the units one metre), present or absent by board. In the record, the board's opening entry carries the opening frame and the unit (`init` with `board: 'wright'`, `unit`, `frame`); every move or resize appends a `frame` entry with the whole frame after it, so Undo steps back through them; Lock appends a `lock` entry, so Undo unlocks. The Wright window's model is now its panes and its cuts, read off the shell's frame; its four sides are edges a line is pulled from exactly as a cut is, and nothing more.

**Moving and resizing.** Nine copper handles, present while the frame is unlocked and no demo plays: one outside the middle of each side (as before), one outside each corner (new; a corner moves two sides), one at the centre (new; it moves the whole frame). Each snaps to whole units; the sides' limits are the Wright window's, 1 to 20 units each way. While a handle is dragged the frame's width, height and area read out as before. A handle put back where it was writes nothing.

**Lock.** A word in the column, under *Glass only · Undo*, present on a board with a frame while the frame is free; tapping it writes the entry and the handles go. A cut locks the frame as before (the board says what counts as built from it). A board without a frame has no Lock row at all.

**The home view fits the frame** with a margin of one unit all round, centred in the drawing box; *Reset view* returns to it, and the resize observer re-homes to it while nothing has been made. On a board without a frame the home view is the plane's default, as before.

**Migration.** A record in the old shape (`init.wright` with the frame inside it; `resize` entries) is rewritten in place at the start of every replay, so the Wright demo and any saved design open as before and, if re-saved, are saved in the new shape. The demo log `models/logs/geo_wright.json` is regenerated by the harness in the new shape; the old file replays through the migration to the same panes, cuts and fills, checked in Node.

**The harness.** `tools/glass-log.js`: a resize writes the `frame` entry; `g.lock()` added. `tools/glass-states/glass-states.html`: the Wright walk gained Lock then Undo on the opening, the centre handle held and put back, the top-left corner held, the right handle held, each put back before release so no entry is written.

## Visible changes, as named

On the Wright window only: the frame can be moved (the centre handle), corner handles, the *Lock* word, and the home view fits the frame (zoom 0.93 where it was 0.72 at 1280 wide). Nothing on Stained Glass Circles or the standalone lab.

## What surprised me

- **An empty row has a margin.** The Lock row, built on every board with the word absent, moved Stained Glass Circles' column down eight pixels; the walk caught it, and the row is now built only where there is a frame.
- **A handle released where it was held writes an entry** — the harness's first version did that and moved the frame under the next state. The lab is right (the hand did not come back); the harness now brings it back.
- **A moved or locked frame counts as work:** Save, Share and Undo appear, and the work-in-progress guard asks before a demo. Before R2 a resize counted the same way. Named so you can rule otherwise.

## Open, not invented

- **How the frame moves.** Ruling 9's vocabulary has no slot for a drag that starts inside a pane, so the move is a ninth handle at the frame's centre. The alternative is dragging the frame's inside while it is unlocked; that would need a slot.
- **Where Lock sits** in the column: a row of its own under *Glass only · Undo*, above *New Open Save*. The series page's column question is still open.
- **Whether a moved or locked frame is work** (above).
- **The frame's limits**, 1 to 20 units, are the Wright window's and may not suit every board; and the home margin of one unit is a guess at "with a margin".
- **The frame's readout rule** is whole numbers until R3; the lines board's `exact` rule will come back with resolution.
- **The Wright tips** still say "the small dots outside the sides" and nothing of the corners, the centre or Lock; the words are yours.
- **The unit** is still the board's (56 world units a step) until R3; the entry's fields are in it.
- Saved Wright designs in a browser library open through the migration; none was in hand to try beyond the demo.
