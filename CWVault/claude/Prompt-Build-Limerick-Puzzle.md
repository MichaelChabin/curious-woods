# Build prompt: Limericks

> Read `CWVault/claude/What-CW-Is.md`, then `cw-deploys/MANIFEST.md` (Page standard), then `CWVault/claude/Rulings-Sept-2026.md` and `CWVault/claude/Story-Voice.md`. Then read the spec, all of it: `CWVault/claude/Limericks-Puzzle.md`. Then open the mockup, `CWVault/claude/mockups/limerick-puzzle-mockup.html`, in a browser and play two or three limericks through, including one finished only with *Next line, please*, and make a postcard. Michael has used the mockup and said yes to it. The build matches its behaviour; it is not a redesign.
>
> This is not a story page. It is a puzzle that hangs in the gallery, so it does not use `template-story.html`, and `check-story.sh` does not apply. It follows the Page standard in everything else (head, title, tab icon, Georgia, the Maya flag via `js/cw-flags.js`).
>
> **1. Pictures.** Fetch two drawings by Edward Lear from *A Book of Nonsense* (1846), public domain: the Old Man with a beard, and the Old Man who said "Hush!" (the bird in the bush). Wikimedia Commons or Project Gutenberg's scans are fine; take the best clean scan. Save the originals and a `SOURCES.md` (where each came from, the licence) in `art-originals/limericks/`, and web copies in `art/stories/limericks/` (or `art/` if that folder pattern doesn't exist; say which). The beard drawing goes in the introduction, where the mockup has its placeholder, at its own proportions. The bush drawing is the bush limerick's picture for *Add the picture* on the postcard. The Niger map is not in this build: Niger has no picture for now, so its postcard has no *Add the picture*. Name that as open in the report.
>
> **2. The page.** `cw-deploys/experiments/limericks.html`, built from the mockup: the parchment, the text-only lines, the faint rules, italic until the line lands in its place, then typed out one line at a time with the cursor and the quiet keys, lines 3 and 4 indented once typed, the bell after the last line is typed, *Next line, please* (each tap places, fixes and types the next line) at the left and *Another one* at the right, with *Share Postcard* and *Keep on my list* on their own line below them, centred, appearing when the poem is whole (*Next line, please* stays in place but fades once there is nothing left to give), and the credit fading in when the poem is whole. Keep the sounds synthesized as the mockup does; no sound files. Take every word from the spec, not from memory: the introduction, the Niger background line, the twelve limericks and their credits exactly as the spec gives them. The page's title is *Limericks*.
>
> **3. Order.** The first time she opens the page on her device: the Lear introduction, then the young lady of Niger. After that the introduction does not show again, and the limericks come in a fresh random order each visit, never the same one twice in a row. Keep the one "seen the introduction" flag in her browser's storage and nothing else; wrap the storage calls so the page still works if storage is unavailable.
>
> **4. Share Postcard.** Use Glass Geometry's Postcard as the model (`active/glass-geometry.html`, the export code around `renderPostcardPNG` and `navigator.share`): Postcard is the share action, it goes through the share sheet where the browser has one, and it falls back to a download where it doesn't. The word on the page is *Share Postcard*. The card holds, from the top: her note (the *Your note* box), the limerick's own picture if she taps *Add the picture* (and only if that limerick has one), then the poem as typed and its credit. She cannot upload a picture of her own. Lift shared code into a shelf file only if it can be used as it is; otherwise keep it in the page and say so.
>
> **5. Her list.** When a poem is whole, *Keep on my list* puts it on her list of limericks; on a poem already there the word reads *Take it off my list*. Once the list has anything on it, *My list* appears at the top right of the page. It opens a window (Glass Geometry's picker window: backdrop, close) listing each kept limerick by its first line with the credit under it; tapping one opens it whole, already typed, with *Scramble it* where *Next line, please* was, which scrambles it to be done again. No dates, no counts, nothing else on the list. It lives in her browser's storage on her device and nowhere else, like the practice list; wrap the storage calls. Kept limericks can still turn up in the random order.
>
> **6. Back.** A plain arrow, ←, at the top left of the page, above the parchment. It goes back to the page she came from, or to the gallery if there is no page to go back to. No page has had a back arrow before; say so in the report, since it may want to become the Page standard's.
>
> **7. Remember.** Not in this build. Leave it off the page (`maya: none`).
>
> **8. Gallery picture and icon.** The gallery picture is a drawn mark, not a picture from any one limerick: five lines on parchment in the limerick's own shape (long, long, short and indented, short and indented, long), in ink, the last line half typed with the cursor showing. Real words or plain strokes, whichever reads better at wall size; try both and pick, and say which. Save it as `art/gallery/limericks-gallery.svg` (or PNG). Frame: dark walnut, about `#5a4632`; frame width 8; size small. The tab icon is a 256-px square PNG of the same mark, `art/icons/limericks-icon-256.png`.
>
> **9. Checks.** Register the page in `MANIFEST.md` and add its line to `00-BOARD.md` at stage 2, Built. Run `tools/check-deploys.sh` and fix what it reports. Open the page at phone width and at desktop width and on a touch screen if you can: drag with a finger and a mouse, a line typed only once it lands in place, a line dragged away mid-typing going back to italic, *Next line, please* finishing a poem on its own, the bell after the last line, the postcard with and without the bush picture and with her note on top, a limerick kept and opened again from *My list*, *Scramble it*, and the back arrow. Anything the spec leaves open, leave open and name it; do not invent. Stop and report before committing: where the drawings came from, which gallery mark you chose, what the checks said, anything that didn't resolve. After Michael's yes, commit and push.
>
> **10. Hanging, only after Michael has read the page at its own address and said to hang it.** Move it from `experiments/` to `active/limericks.html` with a `_redirects` line so the old address still works, add its line to `stories/gallery.json` (slug `limericks`, title `Limericks`, href, the gallery picture, frame, frameWidth, size), move its board line to Hung, commit and push, and check it on curiouswoods.org.
